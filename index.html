Rebuilding AI engineering from first principles

I left my Associate Director role to learn AI properly — real systems, real practice, no shortcuts. With a small study group I've gone from micrograd and makemore through RAG and hybrid search to LangChain agents and MCP, testing everything on my homelab.

I'm a software engineer with over nine years of experience, specialising in backend systems, microservices, and cloud. I like simple, effective solutions built on core computer-science principles.

I've worked everywhere from fast-moving startups to VMware's Professional Services Center of Excellence, delivering business-critical systems for Fortune 500 customers. Along the way I've been a Software Engineer, Senior Engineer, Technical Lead, and most recently Associate Director at Uzabase, working on the SPEEDA Edge market-intelligence platform.

Many people add AI to products without understanding how it works, connect an API and call it done. I want to be the engineer who understands the system: why it works, and when it will break. So I'm going back to basics and learning in public in my digital garden.
